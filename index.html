01 / ABOUT

About 个人简介

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Peking University, co-advised by Assistant Professor Shanghang Zhang. My current work studies how embodied and multimodal systems can remain safe, trustworthy, and accountable in open-world environments. 我现为北京大学博士后研究员，合作导师为仉尚航助理教授。当前研究关注具身与多模态系统如何在开放环境中保持安全、可信与可追责。

My research path connects computer vision with information security: from optical information hiding and blind watermarking to copyright authentication, generated-content traceability, and embodied intelligence safety. 我的研究脉络连接计算机视觉与信息安全，从光学信息隐藏、盲水印，延伸到版权认证、生成内容溯源与具身智能安全。

CURRENT VECTOR Safe agents · Trustworthy models · Verifiable content 安全智能体 · 可信模型 · 可验证内容

02 / RESEARCH

Research Interests 研究方向

R/01
⌬

Embodied Intelligence Safety 具身智能安全

Safe decision-making, continual adaptation, and robust perception for agents acting in physical environments. 面向物理环境智能体的安全决策、持续适应与鲁棒感知。

R/02
◫

Trustworthy Multimodal Models 可信多模态大模型

Reliability, privacy, and generalization across visual, language, and action spaces. 研究视觉、语言与动作空间中的可靠性、隐私保护与泛化能力。

R/03
◇

Copyright Authentication 版权认证与水印

Robust blind watermarking and content authentication under real-world distortions and black-box noise. 面向真实失真与黑盒噪声的鲁棒盲水印和内容认证。

R/04
⌁

Generated-Content Traceability 生成内容溯源

Attribution, provenance, and forensic signals for synthetic images, video, and generative systems. 面向合成图像、视频与生成系统的归因、来源证明和取证信号。
